Everything you need

Packed with features. Built for athletes.

Everything you need to train smarter, stay accountable, and build a fitness community — all in one free app.

📱

Social Feed

Share workouts, progress photos, and achievements. React with custom emojis, comment with media, and follow friends on their fitness journey.

💬

Real-time Chat

Group chats with @mentions, swipe-to-quote, media sharing, voice notes, and per-message emoji reactions. Stay connected between sessions.

🤖

AI Workout & Diet Plans

Get personalised workout and nutrition plans generated by AI based on your goals, fitness level, and schedule. Adapt and progress week by week.

🥗

Nutrition Tracking

Log meals, track macros with visual rings, monitor water intake, and scan foods with AI lookup. Fuel your training with precision.

🏆

Achievements & Streaks

Earn badges for PRs, consistency, and milestones. Build streaks with grace days, celebrate with animated unlocks, and track your progress ring.

👥

Buddy System

Connect with gym buddies, follow their activity, celebrate their wins, and keep each other accountable with a dedicated social layer.

🏋️

Profile & Progress

A rich personal profile with your stats, personal records, workout history, achievements showcase, and a customisable public presence.

⚔️

Challenges

Join community challenges and buddy challenges across different difficulty levels. Earn bonus points, track leaderboards, and push your limits.
